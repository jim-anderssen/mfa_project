# Example data of the top recorded waste shipments for disposal by tonnage

This shows the flow of large volumes of waste for disposal.

"Nordic_shipment_economic_potential" is a query of the top 20 largest recorded shipments of waste for disposal in the dataset, grouped by level 2 EWC-stat code. Recycling potential indices are estimations.

"Nor_Den_Effluent_Sludge_Exp_Waste_For_Disposal.csv" is an example of ungrouping the middle_level and group by 6-digit LoW code, giving the full waste character detail of the dataset.

"EU_shipment_economic_potential" is the same pipeline as the Nordic shipment, but for the whole of the EU.

"All_EU_largest_shipment" is an example of tracking the largest shipments in the EU, including timelines, and could be used for predicting the size of future waste shipments.
